# PROJECT JANUS — Artwork brief (for an image-generation model)

## Master style (prepend to every prompt)
"Dark science-horror management game, painterly semi-realism, near-black backgrounds (#0a1420), cyan (#33d6ff) instrument lighting against deep shadow, sickly green (#66ff99) and blood-red (#ff5544) accents only where noted, film grain, volumetric gloom, no text, no watermark, no UI elements"

## Priority 1 — key art + store
1. **Main capsule (landscape 16:9):** containment laboratory at night, six anomalous objects in glass cells receding into darkness (metallic sphere, basalt rock, hollow iron ring, singing glass pane, frost-covered seed, black torus), three researchers as silhouettes in foreground, one red warning light. Dread, scale, mystery.
2. **Logo lockup:** "PROJECT JANUS" in a cold technical sans-serif, letterspaced, cyan on black, subtle scanline fracture through the letter U.
3. **Hero background (16:9, very dark, low contrast — UI sits on top):** out-of-focus lab depth, bokeh instrument lights, vignette to black at edges.

## Priority 2 — artifact portraits (square 1:1, object centered, black void background)
1. **J001 Lattice Sphere:** seamless metallic sphere, faint gravitational lensing ripple around it, cyan rim light.
2. **J002 Basalt Anomaly:** rough black rock, hairline orange cracks glowing from within.
3. **J003 Iron Hollow Core:** hollow iron ring, green null-glow in the aperture, floating dust motes frozen mid-air.
4. **J004 Choir Glass:** translucent pane with visible golden standing-wave patterns, hairline fractures.
5. **J005 Winter Seed:** seed-shaped object caked in geometric frost, cold blue breath-fog, tea cup frozen solid beside it (story beat).
6. **J006 Obsidian Torus:** black torus, violet 0.4-second signal echo rendered as ghost duplicate offset behind it.

## Priority 3 — people (bust portraits, 3:4, dark background, rim-lit)
- **Dr. Sarah Chen:** tired woman, 30s, safety glasses pushed up, thermal burn scar on one cheek.
- **Dr. Marcus Reed:** broad man, 40s, bandaged forearm, calm defiant stare.
- **Dr. Elena Vasquez:** sharp woman, 30s, headphones around neck, listening expression.
- **Hires:** Ingrid Lund (pale, cryo-gear), Kwame Osei (brilliant, restless energy), Anya Petrova (steady, ex-military bearing).
- **Rival directors (6, menacing boardroom portraits):** Ilsa Rennick (HELIOS, predatory executive), Amos Bermant (avuncular, steel underneath), D. Okafor (academic, disappointed), 'Lucky' Marsh (feral startup grin), A. Solenne (old money, serene), V. Moroz (state officer, winter coat).

## Priority 4 — places & texture
- Laboratory hub (16:9 establishing shot matching key art palette, empty of people).
- Infirmary (clinical white-green, empty gurney, curtain).
- Memorial wall (brass plaques in darkness, one fresh).
- 4 incident spot illustrations (aperture shear, frostbite, resonance deafening, critical exposure) — show aftermath, not the moment; blood restrained but present.
- 12 facility icons (flat, cyan line-art on dark): lab flask, shield, trading chart, eye, scanner, garrison chevron, 6 prize marks (gold variants).

## Palette lock
Backgrounds #0a1420–#050a12 · Primary cyan #33d6ff · Healthy green #66ff99 · Warning amber #ffcc44 · Blood/injury red #ff5544 · Rival HELIOS red, Bermant steel-blue, Northwind green, Vantage gold.

## Avoid
Photoreal gore close-ups, text in images, bright daylight, cartoon or anime styles, cluttered compositions (UI overlays everything).
