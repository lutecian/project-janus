# PLAYTEST GUIDE — PROJECT JANUS

## Version
JANUS 0.01 — Internal/External Playtest Build

## Goal
Test whether the core experimentation loop is compelling. Does the player genuinely want to discover what the artifact does?

## What to observe
1. **Completion rate** — Do testers reach discovery confirmation?
2. **Experiment variety** — Do they try different experiments, or just spam one?
3. **Scientist selection** — Do they notice skill differences? Do they care?
4. **HELIOS pressure** — Does the rival create urgency?
5. **Knowledge state transitions** — Do "unknown → suspected → confirmed" feel meaningful?
6. **Naming moment** — Is naming the discovery satisfying?
7. **Save/load** — Do testers save and reload? Does state persist correctly?

## How to run a playtest
1. Launch game
2. Hand mouse to tester
3. Say: "You are a research director. Investigate this unknown artifact."
4. Do NOT explain mechanics. Let them discover.
5. Watch silently. Take notes.
6. After 20 minutes or discovery, ask:
   - What did you enjoy most?
   - What frustrated you?
   - Would you play more?
   - Did you feel pressure from HELIOS?
   - Did the scientists feel different?

## Balance targets (JANUS 0.01)
- Time to discovery: 15-25 minutes (8-15 experiments)
- HELIOS should reach 60-80% by the time player confirms discovery
- Scientist skill differences should be noticeable (15-20% quality variance)
- Locked experiments should unlock gradually, not all at once
- Critical success should feel special (~5% chance)
- Malfunction should feel rare but impactful (~5% chance)

## Known issues (document if testers hit these)
- No sound
- No animations
- Placeholder UI only
- No multiple artifacts yet
- No resource management
